Ritually constructed from the bones of a nightmare, a nightmare's lament is a +1 striking rapier pistol. Thin wisps of dark smoke continually surround the red-flecked blade.

**Activate—Fuming Cloak** `pf2:2` (manipulate)

**Frequency** once per day

* * *

**Effect** For 1 minute, the nightmare's lament continually vents thick, black smoke that creates concealment in a 15-foot aura around the gun's wielder. The wielder of the nightmare's lament can see through this smoke. A creature that begins its turn in the area becomes [[Conditions/Sickened 1|Sickened 2]] (DC 23 Fortitude negates) and is then temporarily immune to the sickness from the smoke for 1 minute. The wielder of the nightmare's lament, any creature currently holding its breath (or that does not need to breathe), and any creature immune to poison are immune to the aura's sickened effect but not the concealment.

* * *

**Craft Requirements** The initial raw materials must include the rib bones and inner fire of a nightmare.
