To start, you'll all have: 
- 30 gp to spend on common items and gear 
- 1 3rd level permanent item, 
- 2 2nd level permanent items, 
- 1 1st level permanent item. 
- a Wayfinder.
The explanation of rarity in the system is found [here](https://2e.aonprd.com/Rules.aspx?ID=2530) and [here](https://2e.aonprd.com/Rules.aspx?ID=2659). Of note, items that are uncommon but from the Pathfinder Society Guide are probably common for your group, just run them by me. For example, Grappling arrows and bolts fit in this category.
Rules on [bulk](https://2e.aonprd.com/Rules.aspx?ID=2153)and [carrying items](https://2e.aonprd.com/Rules.aspx?ID=2148).

Now onto items!
All items with bonuses to skills can be found [here](https://2e.aonprd.com/ItemBonuses.aspx)

[Adventuring Gear](https://2e.aonprd.com/Equipment.aspx?Category=1&include-rarities=common&sort=level-asc+price-asc+name-asc&display=table&columns=pfs+source+rarity+trait+level+price+bulk+usage+spoilers) is a solid place to look for things to buy.
- [Adventurer's Pack](https://2e.aonprd.com/Equipment.aspx?ID=2700) to start of course, but you may want extra rations at some point. 
- Before you leave town, [tents](https://2e.aonprd.com/Equipment.aspx?ID=2757) are a must.
- [Repair Toolkit](https://2e.aonprd.com/Equipment.aspx?ID=2747) for keeping equipment in working order. The desert is not gentle. 
- Speaking of things that could be repaired, [thieves' toolkit](https://2e.aonprd.com/Equipment.aspx?ID=2758). Though you could bring replacement picks as well. To see the concealable ones (which are 80gp) you have to switch over to the Legacy content. To be fair, you can always try to conceal something small enough, these ones are just better at it on top of being infiltrator thieves' tools.
- Having a [healer's toolkit](https://2e.aonprd.com/Equipment.aspx?ID=2727) is necessary to Treat Wounds!!!
- Depending on what you want to do, a [crowbar](https://2e.aonprd.com/Equipment.aspx?ID=2718)might be helpful.
- A [climbing kit](https://2e.aonprd.com/Equipment.aspx?ID=2713) could come in handy if you're heading to the mountains, or into a canyon.
- There are also classic items like caltrops, marbles, and 10-ft poles.

[Alchemical Items](https://2e.aonprd.com/Equipment.aspx?Category=6&include-rarities=common&sort=level-asc+price-asc+name-asc&display=table&columns=pfs+source+rarity+trait+item_subcategory+level+price+bulk+usage+spoilers) 
- There's lots here that I haven't delved into. There's alcohol (looks like it hasn't been reprinted, so it still says flat-footed instead of off-guard), flayleaf is basically marijuana. Antidotes and similar are here too. 

[Armour](https://2e.aonprd.com/Armor.aspx?include-rarities=common&sort=rarity-asc&display=table&columns=pfs+source+rarity+trait+armor_category+ac+dex_cap+check_penalty+speed_penalty+strength_req+price+level+bulk+armor_group) & [Shields](https://2e.aonprd.com/Shields.aspx)
- Being strong enough for your armour allows you to ignore the check penalty, and takes 5ft off of the speed penalty. 

[Consumables](https://2e.aonprd.com/Equipment.aspx?Category=15&include-rarities=common&sort=level-asc+price-asc+name-asc&display=table&columns=pfs+source+rarity+trait+item_subcategory+level+price+bulk+usage+spoilers) 
- While you can't get consumables as part of your free permanent items, there are some neat sections here to browse. Oils, potions, scrolls, spell catalysts, talismans and more! 
- A note on talismans. I get that they're a bit weak, but the main idea with them seems to hinge on them being usable items that don't require your hands to activate. Attach ahead of time, deploy as you need. 

The [Customizations](https://2e.aonprd.com/Equipment.aspx?Category=75&Subcategory=76) section has some scopes for attaching to firearms and crossbows. Nearly all of them are higher than your current level, but certainly worth being aware of.

[Held Items](https://2e.aonprd.com/Equipment.aspx?Category=21&include-rarities=common&sort=level-asc+price-asc+name-asc&display=table&columns=pfs+source+rarity+trait+level+price+bulk+usage+spoilers) not including Weapons, wands and staves.

[Runes](https://2e.aonprd.com/Equipment.aspx?Category=23&include-rarities=common&sort=level-asc+price-asc+name-asc&display=table&columns=pfs+source+rarity+trait+item_subcategory+level+price+bulk+usage+spoilers) 
- It's certainly necessary to get your +1 Weapon Potency rune on your main weapon if you use one. There are a few level 3 runes to look at as well.

[Snares](https://2e.aonprd.com/Equipment.aspx?Category=31&include-rarities=common&sort=level-asc+price-asc+name-asc&display=table&columns=pfs+source+rarity+trait+level+price+bulk+usage+spoilers) 

[Spellhearts](https://2e.aonprd.com/Equipment.aspx?Category=68)
- very cool items that give you cantrips as well as giving you options in how you put them to use. They give different benefits based on if you attach them to your weapon or armour.

[Staves](https://2e.aonprd.com/Equipment.aspx?Category=32&include-rarities=common&sort=level-asc+price-asc+name-asc&display=table&columns=pfs+source+rarity+trait+level+price+bulk+usage+spoilers) 
- Great for casters looking for more options. Prepared and spontaneous casters can each enhance their usage of a staff in different ways. 

[Tattoos](https://2e.aonprd.com/Equipment.aspx?Category=46&include-rarities=common&sort=level-asc+price-asc+name-asc&display=table&columns=pfs+source+rarity+trait+level+price+bulk+usage+spoilers) 

[Wagon](https://2e.aonprd.com/Vehicles.aspx?ID=72) 
- Duncan does own a wagon. It has 18 HP, and an AC of 11.

[Wands](https://2e.aonprd.com/Equipment.aspx?Category=34&include-rarities=common&sort=level-asc+price-asc+name-asc&display=table&columns=pfs+source+rarity+trait+item_subcategory+level+price+bulk+usage+spoilers) 
- Only one that's <= level 3 is a 1st-rank spell wand. Basically a once-per day cast of a spell, unless you want to risk destroying it for a second cast.

[Weapons!](https://2e.aonprd.com/Weapons.aspx?include-rarities=common&sort=weapon_type-asc+weapon_category-desc+name-asc&display=table&columns=pfs+source+weapon_type+weapon_category+weapon_group+trait+damage+hands+range+reload+bulk+price+level) 
- The weapon group tells you what the weapon does when you crit if you have the critical specialization feature (something your class may give you)
- Traits like Trip and Shove mean the weapon can be used to do that without needing a free hand. 
- Finesse means you can use Dex to attack with the weapon. You still use Strength or nothing on damage though. Dex on damage is a feature for thief rogues. 
- Agile lowers the multiple attack penalty. 
- Deadly adds an extra die to your damage after doubling your normal damage on a crit. If something normally does 1d8 and has deadly d10, then on crit it does 2d8 + 1d10.
- Fatal does the same thing as deadly, but it also changes the die size of your weapons normal damage. If something normally does 1d8 and has fatal d12, then on crit it does 3d12.
