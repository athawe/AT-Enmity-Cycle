Based on historical accounts and records, the court of Alfred the Great (r. 871-899) was characterized by:
- **Royal Council**: A small group of trusted advisors, including nobles, bishops, and high-ranking officials, who played a crucial role in decision-making and governance.
- **Court Scholars**: A team of learned individuals, including translators, scribes, and intellectuals, who worked on translating Latin texts into English, promoting education, and advising the king on matters of culture and learning.
- **Military Commanders**: Experienced military leaders who oversaw the defense of Wessex against Viking invasions and raids.
- **Administrative Officials**: Responsible for managing the kingdom’s finances, taxation, and conscription system, which was based on the “hide” system (tenant’s obligation to the community based on landholding quality).
- **Chaplains and Clerics**: Provided spiritual guidance and support to the king and his court.
