- **Desert travel** is tough and unforgiving
- Both rocky and sandy areas can have difficult terrain or uneven ground. Thorn ignores the nonmagical difficult terrain currently, and ignores the uneven ground at level 11.
- The temperature can reach heights that lower the amount of time you can travel per day without [[Fatigued|fatigue]]. It can even go so high that you take fire damage over time.
- The wind can make auditory Perception checks harder, interfere with physical ranged attacks, and even force Athletics checks to move. 
- Sandstorms, tornadoes and a local type of sandstorm called a khamsin (calm-sin) can pose even greater threats 
- For dealing with food, Duncan's [[Forager]] feat allows him to guarantee success on feeding the whole party using the Subsist action and rolling Survival. Beasts of burden and such will need one additional check (just based off of the numbers) if you don't already have food for them. 
- For water, Jynx's [[CompendiumDirectoryPF2e/Character Building/Feats/Base Kinesis|Base Kinesis]] can pull it from the Elemental Plane of Water, to sate the party's thirst. 
- You can go without either for Con Mod +1 days. If you did, starvation causes 1 damage per day that can't be healed until you eat, and thirst causes 1d4 damage per hour that also can't be healed until you drink.
- Actual hexploration - 
- You get a number of activities per day based on the speed of the slowest person in the group. Camels move 35 feet per round, giving you 2 activities per day. Jynx's [[Efficient Explorer]] increases that to 3 activities while in the desert.
- There are group activities and individual ones.
- Group activities 
- Travel and [[Reconnoiter]] 
- Individual activities 
- Fortify Camp and Map the Area 
- Being lost - As you reach the middle of each hex, someone will need to Sense Direction to keep their bearings, and someone else will use Perception, Nature or Desert Lore to ensure the effectiveness of your travel efforts. Those are in order from highest to lowest DC.
- (My plan is that only 2 consecutive successes in the same direction will clear a hex)
- Fatigue 
- You'll likely deal with being fatigued in the later half of your day, unless you stop at that point. Depending on how well you prepare and how effective your efforts are, you may reduce that down to the last third.
