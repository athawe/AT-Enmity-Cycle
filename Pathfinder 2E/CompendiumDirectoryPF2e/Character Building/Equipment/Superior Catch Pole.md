# Superior Catch Pole
## Item 4
### 


**Price** 80 gp; 
**Bulk** 1; **Size** med
**Usage** held-in-two-hands

A superior catch pole has a pole made of dawnsilver, and the loop at the end is silk rope, making it sturdier and more agile. It functions the same as a catch pole but gives you a +1 item bonus to Athletics checks made to [[Actions/Grapple|Grapple]] with it.

*Source: Pathfinder Howl of the Wild*
