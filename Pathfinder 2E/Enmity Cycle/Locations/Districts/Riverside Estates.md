Description: Along the waterfront, the most elegant and well constructed region of town outside of influential buildings like the Queen's castle and the Serene Spiral. Almost all of the buildings are large mansion size dwellings, the streets perfectly paved and lit, and luscious gardens adorn every inch of available land between them. Many are fenced and gated.
